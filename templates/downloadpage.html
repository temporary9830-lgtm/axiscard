About this app

›

Introducing the enhanced Axis Bank MobileBanking App, your one-stop solution to a seamless and secure banking experience. Experience the convenience of doing 150+ transactions through our MobileBanking app.

Features include:

  • Instant Fund Transfers: Use NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, and UPI instantly.
  • Comprehensive Account Summary: Track your savings, current accounts, and fixed deposits seamlessly.
  • Credit & Debit Cards: Manage limits, block cards, or generate PINs entirely within the app.
  • Instant Loans: Apply for personal loans and credit cards and get instant approvals based on eligibility.
  • Investment Portfolio: Manage Mutual Funds, PPF, and Sovereign Gold Bonds directly from your dashboard.

Security is our top priority. We employ advanced 256-bit encryption to ensure that all your financial data and transactions remain entirely secure at all times.
